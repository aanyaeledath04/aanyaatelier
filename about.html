About the Artist

Hi! I'm a high school student artist based in Austin, Texas, and I've been making art for as long as I can remember. Starting with colored pencils and sketchbooks, I gradually worked my way into mediums like acrylic, watercolor, charcoal, and many more.

This portfolio brings together work from the past several years. It includes studies, gifts made for family, pieces inspired by favorite artists, and experiments in mediums I was trying for the first time. I like moving between styles, and I'm always working on something new, so this collection will keep growing. Thank you for taking the time to look through it!

— Aanya Eledath
